# Related work

## Clean and faulty search

[Grover](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605043) gives the clean reflection algorithm. [Bennett, Bernstein, Brassard, and Vazirani](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9701001) establish the square-root query obstruction by comparing marked and unmarked oracles. [Childs's lecture notes](../learning/CHILDS_BRIDGE.md) develop the two-dimensional geometry and a modern query lower-bound account.

[Regev and Schiff](https://cims.nyu.edu/~regev/papers/faultygrover.pdf), Theorem 1, prove linear search at any fixed independent omission probability. Their model agrees with the entire process at $`\kappa=0`$, and with each proper block marginal for every $`\kappa`$ in the present model. Their proof introduces the pure-substate progress method and explains the change in the direction of rotation when a reflection is omitted. [Nahimovs, Rivosh, and Kravchenko](https://www.bjmc.lu.lv/fileadmin/user_upload/lu_portal/projekti/bjmc/Contents/787_7.pdf), §§3.1–3.2, give a reflection-cancellation analysis for randomly positioned omissions. The [construction](../science/construction.md) uses this geometry under a full-block parity constraint. The [converse](../science/converse.md) treats the signed block operator and unrestricted adaptive interleavings.

## Temporal models

[Quantum Search Memory Laws](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Search-Memory-Laws/tree/55e1d5d34a23e5af0ed1a89628868a4f2e3f0455) gives sharp query and elapsed-slot laws for a hidden two-state Markov activation process. The same work's [independent-pair comparison](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Search-Memory-Laws/blob/55e1d5d34a23e5af0ed1a89628868a4f2e3f0455/science/paired-comparison.md) already proves the length-two positive-parity case and supplies the two-reference phase certificate. In the pair model the activating correlation is present in the two-call marginal. Here every proper marginal, including all pair marginals, is independent, and the optimal cost quantifies the price of exposing the full block as its length grows. The paired simulation by a diluted independent-fault oracle proves its converse at length two. The [factorization into two products of projections](../science/converse.md) handles both signs and growing block length.

[Mandal, Ghoshal, Srivastava, and Sen](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.107.022427) study correlated local unitary errors between ideal Grover iterations. The parity in their qubit-local noise result counts affected qubits. The present parity counts omissions of the marked oracle across time, with ideal known controls and no fault flags.

[Lolck, Mančinska, and Paraashar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04931) give general robustification for independently faulty queries. [Agarwal, Rao, and Song](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00506) treat independent negligence through fractional block sensitivity and study broader time-varying dephasing schedules. Their dephasing oracle performs the query and may then dephase its index register. The omission model here randomly applies the unknown marked reflection or the identity, with correlations hidden from the receiver.

[Pollock, Rodríguez-Rosario, Frauenheim, Paternostro, and Modi](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.040405) describe operational Markov behavior in terms of multitime interventions. The [interpretation](../science/interpretation.md) gives a specific query-complexity consequence of a correlation absent from all proper block reductions. Determining the law uses the promised binary omission process and known block length.
